A Writing Update

10/10/2026


I definitely suffer from many things I want to do and not enough time to do them. There’s a scale of how inspired I am to do something and it’s hard to find inspiration sometimes to write a blog or create a project. But I love writing. It’s fun, and even though I don’t do it as much as I should, that’s ok. There’s a funny thing with ideas in that coming up with an idea is a skill. You can improve it and grow it over time. I have that with project ideas. Since high school, I have grown in this skill and now I'm swarmed with so many ideas that I can't keep up. The goal is to do that with writing.

So hopefully, see you more soon.

- Marius
